The Challenge

The Perils of Martian Surface Navigation

Traversing Mars is fundamentally constrained by rugged planetary geology and harsh operational limits.

> 20°

Impassable Crater Scarps

Delta remnants and crater walls present extreme slopes where rovers risk tip-over and human space suits cannot gain traction.

0.12 km/h

Rover Mobility Limits

Planetary rovers like Perseverance move at slow paces; dead-end scarp traps can cost months of mission time or mission failure.

8 Hours

Astronaut EVA Life Support

Human explorers face strict metabolic oxygen and thermal suit constraints. Every kilometer of unnecessary ascent expends critical reserves.

⚠️ The Core Problem: Straight-line (Euclidean) navigation directly crosses lethal scarps. Planetary explorers require real-time elevation-aware guidance that routes safely through natural saddles.

Algorithmic Engine

Topographic Slope Derivation & Cost A*

Translating elevation matrices into mathematical risk cost surfaces for optimal path execution.

2D Gradient Slope Equation

Instantaneous gradient vectors are derived across the 20m raster grid using central difference convolutions:

Slope(°) = arctan(√((dz/dx)² + (dz/dy)²)) × (180/π)

Classified into 3 tiers: Nominal (<10°), Caution (10°–20°), and Impassable Hazard (>20°).

Cost-Weighted Traversal Function

The graph edge cost balances distance with an exponential slope penalty:

Cost = Distance × (1.0 + 8.0 × (Slope / Limit)²)

If Slope > Limit: Cost = ∞ (Impassable barrier forces path around the ridge).
